Why a 90-day commitment?

Local SEO compounds: the work we do in month one keeps paying off in month six. Ninety days is the minimum window for the early results to show up and for you to judge us fairly. After that, it's month-to-month. Stay because it's working, not because a contract says so.

What happens if I cancel?

Everything stays yours. Your Google Business Profile, your reviews, your directory listings, and every piece of content we created. All of it remains with you, permanently. We'll hand over a final report summarizing everything that was done. No removal fees, no hostage situations.

Are there any other costs?

No. No setup fee, no per-lead charges, no ad spend to fund. $997 a month covers all of it. If we ever recommend something that costs extra (it hasn't happened yet), you'll hear about it before, not after.

How does billing work?

Monthly billing, starting when work begins, not when you sign up. You'll get an invoice each month, and you can pay by card or ACH.
