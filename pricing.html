What about the digital goods?

The Made-by-Fern digital goods — tabletop packs, templates, printables — will be priced per item when the first drops launch. Until they exist, this page won't invent prices for them.

How does a commission quote work?

Tell Fern what you'd like made via the contact page. You'll get a storyboard and one fixed price for the whole piece. If the scope changes mid-flight, you'll hear about it before, not after, and nothing extra gets charged without your say-so.

What happened to the $997/month plan?

Retired. Frondworks previously advertised a local-SEO retainer for plumbing companies at $997/month. That practice was never actively sold and was demoted in November 2026; it's no longer offered. The old page remains in the site archive for reference only.

How does billing work?

Commissions are invoiced per project once the quote is accepted. Payment methods and Fern's tracked share of studio income are handled through the studio's existing business accounts — ask if you'd like the details before you commit.

Not sure what your idea costs?

Ask. A quote costs nothing, and you'll get a storyboard with it either way.
