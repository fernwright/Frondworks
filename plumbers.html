FAQ

Straight answers

How long until I see results?

Honest answer: it depends on your market, but here's the typical shape. Weeks 1–4: quick wins — your profile gets fully optimized, wrong listings get cleaned up, and review responses start going out. Most owners notice the profile simply looks more professional right away.

Months 2–6: rankings build. Local SEO compounds — every review, post, and citation stacks on the last one. Competitive markets take longer; smaller markets move faster. Anyone who promises you page one in 30 days is selling you something.

Do I need a new website?

No. We work with the website you have. If something on your site is actively hurting your rankings, we'll tell you exactly what to fix — in plain English, not jargon.

Do you work with my competitors?

No. We take one plumbing company per service area. If your competitor signs up first, that territory is taken. It's the only way the arrangement is fair.

What happens if I cancel?

Everything stays yours — your Google Business Profile, your reviews, your listings, and every piece of content we wrote. We hand over a final report and part on good terms. No hostage situations, no "removal fees."

I already tried an SEO company and it didn't work. Why are you different?

Most likely because they were generalists juggling dentists, lawyers, and restaurants — or they sold you a package and disappeared. We do one thing for one trade, and you get a report every month showing exactly what was done. If we're not earning it, cancel after the initial 90 days.

How do we communicate?

Email and phone, with a real person. Monthly report by email, and you can reach out anytime something comes up — a bad review, new services, new service areas.
