List every subscription and recurring bill in one spreadsheet for Excel and Google Sheets.

Enter the name, category, payment method, frequency, amount, next renewal, and notes. Monthly and annual costs are calculated from frequency (weekly, monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, or annual).

What it does

  • Keep / Cancel / Downgrade column: tallies possible savings. Cancel uses the full annual cost, downgrade uses the difference from a new amount you enter, and keep records zero. These figures record your choices, not a promise of money saved.
  • 12-month renewal calendar: the year is set on Setup (default 2027) and the calendar shows what is due each month.
  • Dashboard: spend, spend by category with a chart, decision counts, savings found, the top 5 by annual cost, and renewals in the next 30 days.

This is a digital download with a fictional sample file, a blank copy, and an instructions PDF. Personal-use terms are included.

You type the numbers. It does not connect to your bank, detect subscriptions, or send reminders. No macros. No physical item is shipped.
