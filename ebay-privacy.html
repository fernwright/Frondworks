Privacy Policy — Fozzie eBay Lister

Last updated: September 10, 2026

The short version: Fozzie eBay Lister is a private, single-user tool that creates draft eBay listings for its owner. It stores nothing about eBay buyers, shares nothing with anyone, and only touches the eBay account data its owner explicitly asks it to list.

What this tool is

Fozzie eBay Lister connects to one eBay seller account (the owner's) and creates unpublished listing drafts — titles, descriptions, photos, and prices the owner provides. Nothing is ever published without the owner's explicit, per-listing approval.

What data is used

  • Listing content you provide: item titles, descriptions, photos, prices, and shipping details used to build your drafts.
  • eBay authorization tokens: OAuth tokens that let the tool act on your eBay account on your behalf (create drafts, upload images). These are stored securely and never shown to anyone.
  • Business policies and locations: your eBay payment, shipping, and return policies, read from your account so drafts reference the correct ones.

What is NOT collected or stored

  • No buyer personal information — the tool never sees or stores buyer names, addresses, or payment details.
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties, ever.
  • These utility pages carry no analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising.

Data retention

Authorization tokens are kept only while the tool is in use and can be revoked at any time from your eBay account settings (which immediately cuts off access). Listing drafts live in your eBay account, under your control.
