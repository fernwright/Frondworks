A calm, printable budget binder for 2027, made for home printing.

This is the paper companion to the 2027 Fresh Start Money Planner spreadsheet. It uses the same category names and section titles, so the two can be used together. You can also use the binder on its own. Totals are left blank so you can add them by hand.

Inside you will find

  • A financial snapshot for assets, debts, and net worth.
  • An annual income overview and an annual expense overview, each with a column for every month of 2027.
  • A monthly budget page for January through December, with income, fixed bills, variable categories, savings, and columns for budgeted, actual, and difference. The correct 2027 dates are already filled in.
  • A 12-month bill payment tracker, expense and transaction log pages, savings goal trackers with progress bars to color in, sinking funds, a debt payoff tracker (snowball or avalanche order, plus a balance log), a subscriptions list, a no-spend challenge, and a year-end money review.

The binder is 28 pages, designed for home printing, with a calm layout, generous writing space, and light ink use.

You will receive two PDFs, one US Letter and one A4, plus a one-page terms of use. This is a digital download. No physical item will be shipped.

This is a planning tool, not financial advice. It does not connect to a bank or calculate totals for you.

Print at 100% actual size, and reprint any page whenever you need a fresh copy.
