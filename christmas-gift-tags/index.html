Inside this 7-page pack you will find

  • A cover and a How to Print and Cut guide with a clickable list of sheets.
  • Four color families of hanging tags, with nine tags on each sheet: Cream, Sage, Kraft-Tone, and Charcoal. Each designed tag has a simple motif, a short greeting (such as "Merry Christmas", "Joy", or "For You"), hole-punch marks, and To and From lines.
