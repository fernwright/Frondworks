This planner is an organizing tool. It does not offer nutrition advice, health guidance, or meal plans to follow, and it does not make savings claims. Totals are left blank so you can add them by hand.

Inside you will find

  • Two weekly meal plan pages, each with Monday to Sunday, plus breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks, a week-of date, and a notes and prep area. These pages are meant to be reprinted, and two copies are included.
  • Two grocery lists, sorted by store section: Produce, Meat and Seafood, Dairy and Eggs, Bakery, Pantry, Frozen, Beverages, Household, and Other.
  • A pantry, fridge, and freezer inventory, with space for an item, a quantity, and a date or use-by.
  • A full-page recipe card, plus two pages of 4 x 6 inch recipe cut-out cards, with cut lines. Each cut-out card is 4 inches by 6 inches and sits inside the printable margins.

  • The grocery spending tracker uses the same Groceries category as the 2027 Budget Binder, so the two can be used together. Columns are included for budget, spent, and difference.
