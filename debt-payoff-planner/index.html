A calm, printable planner for organizing debts in 2027, made for home printing.

This planner is an organizing tool, not financial advice. It does not calculate payoff dates or interest, and it does not tell you which method to use. Totals are left blank so you can add them by hand.

Inside you will find

  • A debt inventory, with space for the creditor, type, balance, interest rate, minimum payment, and due date.
  • A plain-language page on two common orders, snowball and avalanche, plus an ordering worksheet. Snowball orders debts from the smallest balance to the largest. Avalanche orders them from the highest interest rate to the lowest.
  • Six debt payoff trackers, each with a balance log and a small progress bar you can color in. Reprint a tracker if you have more than six debts.
  • A 100-square color-in progress chart for your starting total debt, a monthly payment schedule for January to December 2027, an interest and fees log, a page for optional milestone celebrations, and a year-end debt review.

The planner is 15 pages, designed for home printing, with a calm layout, generous writing space, and light ink use.

You will receive two PDFs, one US Letter and one A4, plus a one-page terms of use. This is a digital download. No physical item will be shipped.
