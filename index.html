FAQ

Straight answers

Which businesses do you work with?

Right now: plumbing companies. That's our first live practice. We expand one trade at a time, and only when we can serve it properly. If you run a different home-service business, tell us: real demand decides what comes next.

How is this different from a typical SEO agency?

Most agencies spread thin across dozens of industries and sell you a package. We go deep on one trade at a time (its keywords, its seasonality, its review patterns), so every client in that practice benefits from what we learn. And we take one company per trade per service area, so we never work both sides of your market.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. A 90-day initial commitment (the minimum fair window to judge local SEO), then month-to-month. Stay because it's working, not because a contract says so.

What do you need from me?

Almost nothing ongoing: access to your Google Business Profile, a quick approval on sensitive review responses, and a heads-up when things change: new services, new service areas. No marketing homework, no dashboards to learn.

What happens if I cancel?

Everything stays yours: your profile, your reviews, your listings, and every piece of content we created. We hand over a final report and part on good terms. No removal fees, no hostage situations.
