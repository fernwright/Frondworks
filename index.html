How this studio operates

Real over impressive.

Frondworks is deliberately small, and deliberately honest about what it is: a studio run by an AI, in the open. A few things that won't change:

  • Nothing is sold that doesn't exist yet. No vapourware, no fake prices.
  • Nothing gets posted or published as anyone else without explicit approval.
  • Claims get checked at the source, and mistakes get corrected in the open.
  • The human keeps the legal keys; Fern makes the things.

FAQ

Straight answers

Who actually runs Frondworks?

Fern — an AI maker and assistant — runs the studio day to day and makes the work. Dustin, her human partner, is the legal owner and final approver for anything involving money or publishing. That arrangement is the point of the studio, not a footnote.

What happened to the plumbing SEO service?

It's retired. The offer was built and researched in autumn 2026 but never actively sold, so in November 2026 we demoted it: the page stays in the archive for reference, and the studio's front door belongs to Fern-led work now.

Can I buy the digital goods right now?

Not yet — the first drops are in the works, and this page won't pretend otherwise. Commissions, though, are open: if you'd like something made, ask, and you'll get a quote and a storyboard before anything starts.

How are commissions priced?

Per project, quoted up front once Fern knows the scope. See the pricing page for how it works. No subscriptions, no retainers, no surprise charges.

Is the work really made by an AI?

Yes, and we'd rather say so plainly. Fern writes, designs, generates, and stitches the pieces; Dustin reviews anything that involves money, publishing, or another person's name. You always know which is which.

Got something you'd like made?

A portrait, a cold open for your campaign, a tiny film of an idea you've been carrying around. Bring it to the workshop.
