About

Frondworks makes practical digital downloads: spreadsheets for Excel and Google Sheets and printable PDFs for planning your money, your year, and your goals. You type your own numbers, and nothing connects to your bank.

How do I get my files?

Checkout is handled by Payhip. After you pay, you get an instant download of a zip file. No physical item is shipped.

What formats are the files?

The Money Planner and Bill Audit are Excel workbooks that also work in Google Sheets, with instructions PDFs. The Goal-Setting Kit and Christmas and Holiday Planner are printable PDFs in US Letter and A4. The Monthly and Weekly Planner includes four PDFs: US Letter and A4, in Sunday start and Monday start editions. The Budget Binder is a 28-page printable PDF in US Letter and A4. The Holiday Gift List and Budget Tracker is a free two-page printable PDF in US Letter and A4. The Gift Card Holders and DIY Gift Coupons pack is a 15-page printable PDF in US Letter and A4. The Debt Payoff Planner is a 15-page printable PDF in US Letter and A4. The Meal Planner and Grocery List is a 14-page printable PDF in US Letter and A4. The 2027 Printable Calendar is a free 13-page printable PDF in four editions: US Letter and A4, Sunday start and Monday start. The 2027 Fresh Start Bundle includes the Money Planner, the Bill Audit, and the Goal-Setting Kit in one purchase.

Can I share or resell the files?

No. Each product is licensed for personal use: you may use, edit, and print it for yourself and your household. Please do not resell, share, or redistribute the files. The terms are included in each download.

Do the spreadsheets connect to my bank?

No. You type the numbers. The spreadsheets do not connect to your bank or send reminders, and they use no macros.
