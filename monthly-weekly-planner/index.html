A calm, dated calendar for 2027, made for home printing.

You get four planner PDFs: US Letter and A4, each in a Sunday start edition and a Monday start edition. Print the one that matches your paper and the day you want each week to begin.

Each file is 72 pages and includes

  • A how-to page, and a 2027 year-at-a-glance with all 12 months.
  • A monthly calendar for January through December, with the correct 2027 dates already filled in. US federal holidays are marked on their calendar dates, not on an observed weekday when that is different.
  • A weekly page for every week of 2027 (53 pages), with space for notes and a to-do list. Weeks at the start and end of the year include a few days from late 2026 or early 2028, so each week is a complete seven days.
  • Two notes pages and two project pages at the back.

In a PDF reader, the year overview links to each month, each month links to its weeks, and each week links back. Bookmarks follow the same structure.

This is a digital download, plus a one-page terms of use. No physical item is shipped. Print at 100% actual size, and reprint any page whenever you need a fresh copy.
