A two-page printable for planning holiday gifts and keeping the spending in view.

Page 1 is a gift list. An overall budget box sits at the top, with spaces for the total spent and the amount remaining. Each row has room for the recipient, the gift idea, the planned budget, and the actual cost, plus checkboxes for bought, wrapped, and given. A totals row at the bottom is ready for you to add the columns by hand.

Page 2 has a budget summary by person, group, or category, a gift-ideas brainstorm, and a stocking-stuffer list.

What you get

  • A gift list page with an overall budget, spaces for the amount spent and remaining, and rows for each gift.
  • A second page with a budget summary, a gift-ideas brainstorm, and a stocking-stuffer list.
  • Two PDFs, one US Letter and one A4, plus a one-page terms of use.

The pages are laid out for home printing, with a calm design and light ink use. This is a digital download. No physical item will be shipped.
